Why buyers are looking at Moshi

Moshi is a well known part of the Pimpri Chinchwad region, home to the Pune International Exhibition and Convention Centre and located along the Pune Nashik highway corridor. It sits close to industrial and commercial belts, educational institutions and growing residential clusters. For buyers, that mix usually means steady housing demand from working families, students and professionals.

Dudulgaon: a quieter pocket next to Moshi

Dudulgaon lies beside Moshi and Charholi, near the Indrayani River. Raahee Parna is set in this area, offering a calmer environment than many central locations while schools, hospitals, shopping and IT parks remain within roughly 15 minutes. Points to check before you invest

Whatever the project, do your homework:

  • Verify the RERA registration on the official MahaRERA website
  • Review the developer's track record and delivered projects
  • Compare the carpet area and total package, including stamp duty, registration and GST
  • Visit the site at different times to judge traffic and surroundings
  • Read the agreement carefully and understand payment schedules and maintenance charges
