Raahee Parna Locality and Connectivity: Living in Dudulgaon, Near Moshi

By Raahee Corp · 27 October 2026 · 3 min read

Choosing a home is as much about the neighbourhood as the flat itself. Raahee Parna is located in Dudulgaon, close to Moshi in the Pimpri Chinchwad (PCMC) region of Pune, near the Indrayani River. This guide explains what the locality offers and how connected Raahee Parna is to daily needs. All travel times are approximate and are shown on the project location map.

A calm riverside address in PCMC

Dudulgaon sits between the established areas of Moshi and Charholi, with Alandi to the east. Raahee Parna enjoys a calmer setting than the busier parts of Pune while staying close to markets and services. The nearby Indrayani River gives the area a green, open feel that is rare in a city address.

Schools near Raahee Parna

Families with children will find a good number of schools within a short drive of Raahee Parna:

  • Aditya International School: about 3 to 5 minutes
  • New Orchid International School: about 4 to 7 minutes
  • Priyadarshani School: about 7 to 10 minutes
  • Radcliffe School, Chovisawadi, Silver Birch International School and City Pride School: about 8 to 12 minutes
  • Apex International School and RKL Galaxy International School, Wadmukhwadi: about 10 to 15 minutes

Hospitals and healthcare

Healthcare is close at hand. Kamlesh Multispeciality Hospital and Jijau Mother and Child Care Hospital are about 3 to 5 minutes from Raahee Parna, and Dhanashree Multispeciality Hospital in Moshi is about 5 to 7 minutes away. Bliss Hospital, Tulip Hospital and Sunrise Hospital are within roughly 5 to 10 minutes, and larger facilities such as Accord Hospitals are about 15 to 20 minutes away.

Shopping and daily needs

For groceries and shopping, DMart and Reliance Trends and Digital are about 5 to 10 minutes from the project. For a larger outing, Town Plaza, Silverian Mall and Spine City Mall are roughly 10 to 15 minutes away. This means weekly shopping and weekend entertainment do not require a long trip.

Colleges and education

Students are well served too. Rajmata Jijau Shikshan Prasarak Mandal College of Pharmacy, Dudulgaon is only 2 to 3 minutes away, while MIT Arts, Commerce and Science College, Modern College of Pharmacy and Dnyanvilas College of Engineering are about 5 to 10 minutes. Dr D Y Patil Technical Campus is around 15 to 20 minutes, and Ajeenkya DY Patil University is about 20 to 30 minutes away.

Connectivity to workplaces

Working professionals can reach several employment hubs from Raahee Parna. The Business Gate is about 5 to 10 minutes away, Charholi IT Park about 10 to 15 minutes, and Talwade IT Park about 30 to 40 minutes. Hinjewadi IT Park and Kharadi are farther, at about 60 to 70 minutes depending on traffic, so Dudulgaon suits those who work in the PCMC belt or can work flexibly.

Why the location works for Raahee Parna residents

Taken together, the schools, hospitals, shopping and workplaces within about 15 minutes make Raahee Parna a practical choice for families who want a peaceful campus without giving up convenience. Before buying, we suggest visiting at different times of day to experience the traffic and the neighbourhood for yourself.

Planning a visit to Raahee Parna

When you visit Dudulgaon, drive the routes you would use every day: to your office, to your child's school and to the nearest hospital. Try the trip at peak hours as well as on a weekend, and walk around the neighbouring streets to see the shops and services for yourself.
