Amenities at Raahee Parna, Dudulgaon: A Guide to 40+ Lifestyle Features

By Raahee Corp · 27 October 2026 · 3 min read

Raahee Parna is a residential project by Raahee Corp in Dudulgaon, near Moshi in PCMC, Pune. Spread over 3.5 acres with 7 towers of 14 floors and 686 homes in 2, 2.5 and 3 BHK layouts, the project is planned around more than 40 lifestyle amenities. This guide walks through what the amenity plan at Raahee Parna offers and which members of the family benefit most from each part.

Club Parna: the heart of the community

At the centre of Raahee Parna is Club Parna, a clubhouse planned as the place where neighbours meet. It brings together a multi purpose hall, a cafeteria, a library and a co working space, so everyday needs such as a quiet hour with a book or an important work call do not require leaving the campus. Outside, the swimming pool has separate pools for kids and adults, and the poolside lounge is designed for relaxed evenings.

Sports and recreation at Raahee Parna

Active families have plenty of choice. The sports plan includes a cricket turf, badminton court, squash court, basketball court, climbing wall, table tennis and carrom, along with a riffle shooting area. A jogging track and skating rink connect the activity zones, giving children and adults a safe place to run, skate and play inside the campus.

Health and wellness

Wellness facilities at Raahee Parna cover both workouts and relaxation:

  • An AC gym and fitness area for regular training
  • A zumba studio for group classes
  • Yoga and meditation spaces for a calmer start or end to the day
  • An open air gymnasium and an oxygen park among the greenery
  • An acupressure pathway for a simple barefoot walk

Together they let residents build a routine at home, whether that is a morning walk or an evening class.

Spaces for children, seniors and gatherings

Raahee Parna keeps every age group in mind. The children's play area gives kids a landscaped corner of their own, while the senior citizen sit out offers comfortable seating for conversation. The amphitheatre, party lawn and open party lawn, along with the gazebo, BBQ area, camping fire area and selfie point, make festivals, birthdays and weekend get-togethers easy to host without booking an outside venue.

Nature, landscape and a temple

Greenery is part of the design. A landscape garden and walking pathways run through the campus, and a temple within the project gives families a place for daily prayer and festivals. Being close to the Indrayani River adds to the calm character of the neighbourhood in Dudulgaon.

Everyday convenience and security

Modern living depends on small conveniences, and Raahee Parna plans for them:

  • A Wi Fi zone and co working space for those who work from home
  • A common car wash centre and a driver room
  • EV charging facility and an EV van for society use
  • Provision for a salon and a day care centre
  • A common cooking area for functional activities

Security and safety are covered by a main gate with secure entrance, CCTV in common areas, video door phones, power backup, fire safety systems and a garbage chute. These features matter most when a family is away at work or school, because the campus keeps running smoothly.

Why amenities matter when choosing a home

A long amenity list is only useful if residents actually use it. When comparing projects in Dudulgaon and Moshi, look at how close the facilities are to your tower, whether there are spaces for every age group, and how the project will be maintained over time. At Raahee Parna the amenities sit within a single 3.5 acre campus, so they stay within walking distance of every home.
